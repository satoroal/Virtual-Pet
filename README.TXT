PROJECT TITLE: Animal Companion
PURPOSE OF PROJECT: In Animal Companion you have the ability to create an interactive pet
in the comfort of your own home. You will need to take care of your companion by keeping 
up with their three core statistics - hunger, energy, and mood. You have the ability to 
play, feed, and put your pet to sleep. With this program, you get the responsibility 
and experience of taking care of a pet without the weight and responsibility of 
maintaining and keeping up with a real life.
VERSION or DATE: 09/06/2026
HOW TO START THIS PROJECT: 
To start in Animal Companion:
- Ensure that the VirtualPet.java and PetHouse.java are compiled in blueJ
- Instantiate the virtual window
- Create a new PetHouse()
- Click the "Create" on the left side panel to begin your program
- Name your pet a name which fall within the 1-15 letter requirement
- Once your pet has been created, take care of them as you please!
AUTHORS: Allison Satoro
USER INSTRUCTIONS:
- A safety check has been created to ensure that a pet has been created before
you can begin any interactions, ensure you click the "Create" button before
attempting any interactions
- If you wish to exit the program, click the "Quit" button in the left side panel
